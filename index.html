Dussehra Mela Online

Mela chaloge mere sath? Explore a free 3D Indian nighttime fair in your browser.

Play balloon shooting and ring toss, visit toy and chaat stalls, ride the ferris wheel, watch Ram Leela and celebrate a virtual Ravana Dahan with fireworks.

Use WASD or arrow keys to walk on desktop, or the touch joystick on mobile. All games and virtual souvenirs are free.
